02 / Our Work

One brand.
Five digital disciplines.

01
Build

Website Development

A responsive commerce experience built around easy collection discovery, product filtering, wishlist, cart and enquiry actions. The layout keeps large jewellery visuals at the centre without compromising usability.

  • Collection-first information architecture
  • Responsive e-commerce interface
  • Product, wishlist and cart journeys
02
Discover

SEO

The content hierarchy was organised around the way people actually search for jewellery — by metal, product type, wearer, purity, weight and price — making the catalogue easier for both customers and search engines to understand.

  • Search-led category architecture
  • SEO-friendly product taxonomy
  • Metadata and on-page content direction
03
Engage

Social Media Marketing

A content direction designed to balance product aspiration with everyday relevance — from collection launches and festive moments to styling, gifting and trust-led communication.

  • Campaign and content planning
  • Festive and product storytelling
  • Reels, statics and carousel systems
04
Present

Photography & Visual Direction

Jewellery needs detail, scale and shine to translate on screen. The visual direction focuses on refined backgrounds, consistent framing and close-up product presentation that makes every piece feel premium.

  • Product and campaign visual direction
  • Composition and styling guidance
  • Image selection and digital optimisation
05
Grow

Digital Marketing

The marketing layer connects product campaigns with high-intent journeys — helping seasonal offers, collection launches and trust propositions reach the right audience through conversion-focused creative.

  • Campaign creative and landing direction
  • Audience and retargeting strategy
  • Offer-led conversion communication
03 / Experience Architecture

Built around the way customers shop.

Instead of treating the catalogue as one long product list, the experience guides customers through meaningful choices and reinforces confidence at every step.
